Good to know

Before you arrive

Hours

Lunch Mon–Fri 11:30am–3pm · Aperitivo Mon–Fri 4–7pm · Dinner Mon–Thu 5:30–10pm, Fri–Sat 5:30–11pm, Sun 5–8:30pm · Brunch Sun 12–4pm.

Parties of nine or more

Our private dining team handles larger groups. The private room seats 36, and the 100-seat dining room is available for buyouts.

Dress code

Smart casual. Shorts, hats, tank tops, flip flops, and team or athletic attire are not permitted.

Corkage

Up to two 750ml bottles of wine per table, at $75 corkage per bottle. The cellar holds roughly 1,700 bottles.

Service charge

An 18% service charge applies to parties of 10 or fewer, and 23% for parties of 11 or more.

Parking

Valet parking is available at The Plaza Coral Gables, with the restaurant entrance on Ponce de Leon Boulevard.
