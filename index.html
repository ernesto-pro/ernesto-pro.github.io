The chef

Michael White,
six Michelin stars.

The chef behind New York’s Marea and a James Beard Award winner now cooks in Coral Gables. Wisconsin roots, seven years in Italy and a lifetime of pasta — served without pretense.

MIKA is his first stand-alone restaurant in Florida, opened on December 12, 2024 with partner Alex Pirez. He is often in the kitchen, teaching the next generation and cooking the dishes that made his name.

The house

A love letter to the French Riviera, written in Coral Gables.

MIKA is Chef Michael White’s first stand-alone restaurant in Florida — a warm, sunlit room where the Italian coast meets the easy elegance of the Mediterranean.

Arched plaster, olive trees and rattan pendants set the scene. On the table: crudo and caviar, pasta made by hand, whole fish and a cellar built for long lunches. It is fine dining without the formality — truffles and blue jeans.
