The Story

A sun-warmed room on Ponce de Leon, built around a simple idea: the generosity of the Riviera, at home in Coral Gables.

Courtesy of MIKA

The dining room at The Plaza. Pablo Enriquez / Bishop Design, courtesy of The Spaces

The idea

Fine dining, without the formality.

MIKA is a restaurant that feels like a long lunch on the Côte d’Azur — linen, olive trees, a Negroni in hand — translated into the warmth of a Coral Gables evening.

Chef Michael White built the menu around the coastal kitchen he knows best: crudo and caviar, pasta rolled by hand, whole fish, dry-aged steaks and vegetables treated with the same care as the main event. The room, by Bishop Design, softens the edges with plaster arches, rattan pendants and amber light.

At 8,195 square feet, MIKA holds a 100-seat dining room, a 36-seat private dining room, a marble bar and lounge, and a temperature-controlled cellar of roughly 1,700 bottles. It is generous in scale, intimate in feel.

Chef Michael White. Daniel Zuliani / Courtesy of MIKA

The chef

Michael White,
six Michelin stars.

One of America’s most decorated Italian chefs, Michael White is the force behind New York’s Marea and a James Beard Award winner.

Across his career he has earned six Michelin stars, built restaurants in New York, Las Vegas and beyond, and mentored a generation of pasta makers. MIKA, which opened on December 12, 2024, is his first stand-alone restaurant in Florida.

His cooking is precise but never stiff: a spaghetti finished with blue crab and Siberian sturgeon caviar, a whole branzino under salmoriglio, a bone-in veal chop with stracciatella. The house phrase says it best — truffles and blue jeans.

The room

Bishop Design’s Mediterranean canvas.

Bishop Design drew on the light and texture of the Mediterranean: arched plaster, olive trees indoors, caned chairs, marble, amber velvet and a cascade of crystal above the bar.
