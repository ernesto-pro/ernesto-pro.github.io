Reservations

How do I reserve a table at MIKA Coral Gables?

Book instantly on OpenTable, reserve on Resy, or call us at +1 305 404 2200. The Reserve button on this site opens live OpenTable availability without leaving the page.

How far in advance can I book?

Reservations open 90 days in advance on Resy. OpenTable shows live availability for the same window; if your date is not yet open, check back or call the restaurant.

Can I book for a large party?

Parties of nine or more are handled by our private dining team. MIKA has a 36-seat private dining room and can arrange larger buyouts of the main dining room. Call +1 305 404 2200 or email hello@mikacoralgables.com.

What is the dress code?

Smart casual. Shorts, hats, tank tops, flip flops, and team or athletic attire are not permitted in the dining room.

Can I bring my own wine?

Yes. Up to two 750ml bottles of wine per table may be brought in, with a $75 corkage fee per bottle.

Is there a service charge?

An 18% service charge applies to parties of 10 or fewer, and 23% for parties of 11 or more.

Where do I park?

Valet parking is available at The Plaza Coral Gables. The restaurant is at 3007 Ponce de Leon Blvd, Coral Gables, FL 33134.

The food

What kind of food does MIKA serve?

Coastal Italian and Mediterranean cooking: crudo and caviar, handmade pasta, whole fish, veal, dry-aged steaks and seasonal contorni, with a deep Italian and French wine list.

Who is the chef?

MIKA is the Coral Gables restaurant from Chef Michael White, the Michelin-starred chef behind Marea in New York. Over his career he has earned six Michelin stars and a James Beard Award.

When did MIKA open?

MIKA opened on December 12, 2024 at The Plaza Coral Gables, as Chef Michael White’s first stand-alone restaurant in Florida.

What menus are available?

Dinner, Lunch, Sunday Supper with brunch, Aperitivo Hour and the seasonal Miami Spice menu. The full menus, with prices, are published on our Menu page.

Do you accommodate allergies and dietary restrictions?

Please tell your server about any allergies or dietary restrictions before ordering. Dishes are prepared in a shared kitchen, so we cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment. Raw and undercooked items are marked on the menu.

What is Miami Spice at MIKA?

During Miami Spice, MIKA offers a three-course prix fixe menu for $65, Sunday through Thursday, usually from August through October. The current Miami Spice menu is listed with the other menus.

What is Aperitivo Hour?

Monday through Friday from 4pm to 7pm in the bar and lounge, with small bites, cocktails, wine and beer. It is the easiest way to experience MIKA before dinner.

How many wines do you have?

The temperature-controlled cellar holds roughly 1,700 bottles, with a focus on Italian and French producers and Champagne. Ask the sommelier for a pairing.

Private events

Can I host a private event at MIKA?

Yes. The private dining room seats 36 guests in about 600 square feet and includes a dedicated outdoor area. Larger events can buy out the 100-seat main dining room, and the bar and lounge are available for receptions.

What kind of events can MIKA host?

Seated dinners, receptions, corporate dinners, milestone birthdays, engagement celebrations and full restaurant buyouts. Menus and beverage pairings are tailored to the occasion.

How do I start a private event inquiry?

Call +1 305 404 2200 or email hello@mikacoralgables.com with your preferred date, guest count and occasion. Our events team will follow up with availability and menus.

Visit

Where exactly is MIKA?

MIKA is at The Plaza Coral Gables, 3007 Ponce de Leon Blvd, Coral Gables, FL 33134 — on Ponce de Leon Boulevard, near Malaga Avenue.

What are the opening hours?

Lunch Monday–Friday 11:30am–3pm. Aperitivo Monday–Friday 4–7pm. Dinner Monday–Thursday 5:30–10pm, Friday–Saturday 5:30–11pm, Sunday 5–8:30pm. Brunch Sunday 12–4pm. Hours can change on holidays; call ahead.

Is there a gift card?

Yes. MIKA gift cards can be purchased online through Toast and redeemed at the restaurant. See the Gift Cards page for the direct link.
