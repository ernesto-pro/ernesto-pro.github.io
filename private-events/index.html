The private room

Intimate by design, generous by nature.

The private dining room at MIKA seats 36 in roughly 600 square feet, with its own marble bar and a dedicated outdoor area.

It is a room that works as hard as you do: boardroom by day, candlelit dinner by night, with service that reads the table and menus built around the season. For larger occasions, the entire 100-seat dining room is available for buyout.

Our team will help with the menu, wine pairings, seating and the small details that make a room feel like yours. Tell us the occasion; we will build the evening around it.

Occasions

What we host.

MIKA is built for gatherings that deserve a proper table — and a kitchen with the range to match.

Private events are handled directly by our team. Call +1 305 404 2200 or email hello@mikacoralgables.com.

Seated dinners

Three- and four-course menus, family-style tables or a chef’s tasting, with wine pairings from the 1,700-bottle cellar.

Receptions

Aperitivo-style service in the bar and lounge: crudo, caviar, pasta bites and a dedicated cocktail list.

Corporate

Boardroom dinners, client entertaining and holiday parties, with discreet service and a private entrance to the room.

Celebrations

Birthdays, engagements and anniversaries, with custom menus and the room dressed for the occasion.
