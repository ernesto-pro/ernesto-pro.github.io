Before you write

Quick answers.

More detail on the full FAQ, from dress code to corkage.

Can I book a table through this form?

Yes. Choose “Reserve a table” and the form opens live OpenTable availability without leaving this page. You can also book on Resy or call us directly.

What about parties of nine or more?

Large parties are handled by our private dining team. Choose “Private event”, tell us the guest count and date, and we will come back with the right room and menu.

How quickly will I hear back?

Within one business day. For same-day requests — a table tonight, a change to an existing booking — please call +1 305 404 2200 and we will help immediately.

Do you take press and collaboration requests?

Yes. Choose “Press & media” and include your outlet, angle and deadline. For imagery or interviews we will connect you with the right person.

Skip the form.
