Good to know

Before you order

Lunch prix fixe

Two courses for $42 or three for $48, Monday–Friday from 11:30am to 3pm. Supplements apply to selected dishes.

Cellar & corkage

A 1,700-bottle cellar. Up to two 750ml bottles per table may be brought in for $75 corkage per bottle.

Large parties

Parties of nine or more are handled by our private dining team. The private room seats 36; buyouts are available.

Service charge

An 18% service charge applies to parties of 10 or fewer, and 23% for parties of 11 or more.

Please tell your server about any allergies or dietary restrictions. Raw and undercooked items are marked “Raw.” Menus and prices change with the season and may differ from the published version.
