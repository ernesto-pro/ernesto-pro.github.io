The chef

From Beloit to the Riviera.

Michael White has spent more than three decades cooking Italian food with the discipline of a French kitchen and the ease of a Sunday lunch on the coast.

He grew up in Beloit, Wisconsin, trained at Kendall College in Chicago, and spent seven years at Ristorante San Domenico in Imola — the kind of apprenticeship that shapes a chef for life. In New York he opened Fiamma Osteria, co-founded the Altamarea Group and built Marea into one of America’s defining Italian seafood restaurants.

MIKA is his first stand-alone restaurant in Florida. He opened it on December 12, 2024 at The Plaza Coral Gables with partner Alex Pirez and operator Matt Safchik, and he is often in the kitchen — cooking, teaching and tasting. The name is an Italian nickname for Michael, and also the name of Pirez’s daughter: a restaurant built around family, from the first line of the menu to the last table on a Saturday night.

  8. 2010

    Ai Fiori opens at The Langham and earns a Michelin star. Marea wins Best New Restaurant at the James Beard Awards.

    Publishes Classico e Moderno: Essential Italian Cooking with Andrew Friedman. Costata and Ristorante Morini also join the group.

  10. 2015

    Opens a French restaurant on the Upper East Side, extending the French thread that runs through his Italian cooking.

  11. 2021

    A new chapter

    Leaves the Altamarea Group after more than a decade at the helm and begins building a new portfolio with BBianco Hospitality.

  12. 2022

    Opens Paranza at Atlantis Paradise Island, a coastal Italian restaurant named for the small fishing boats of the Mediterranean.

  14. 2024

    Opens Levant at La Concha Resort in Puerto Rico, cooking Mediterranean food through the lens of his Italian training.

The philosophy

Simplicity is the hardest thing.

White’s cooking is often described as Italian, but the discipline behind it is broader: ingredient first, technique second, ego last. “If it doesn’t add to the dish, we take it out,” he says.

Subtract, don’t add

“Keep it simple and let the ingredients shine,” White says. If an element does not make the dish better, it comes off the plate.

Make it by hand

Pasta is rolled, cut and shaped in house every day. The kitchen is a place to teach, not just to execute.

Truffles and blue jeans

Fine ingredients without the formality. MIKA is designed to be a neighborhood restaurant you can return to, not only a special-occasion room.

Full MIKA FAQ
Who is Chef Michael White?

Michael White is an American chef from Beloit, Wisconsin, known for Coastal Italian cooking and for the New York restaurants Fiamma Osteria, Marea and Ai Fiori. He earned six Michelin stars across his New York restaurants, won the James Beard Award for Best New Restaurant with Marea in 2010, and is the chef and owner of MIKA in Coral Gables, Florida.

Where is Michael White cooking now?

Michael White is Chef & Owner of MIKA at The Plaza Coral Gables, which opened on December 12, 2024. He is often in the MIKA kitchen, and he also cooks at Santi in New York, Paranza at Atlantis Paradise Island, Levant in San Juan and The Lambs Club in New York.

What is Michael White’s connection to Marea?

White co-founded the Altamarea Group with Ahmass Fakahany and opened Marea on Central Park South in 2009. Marea earned two Michelin stars, three stars from The New York Times and the 2010 James Beard Award for Best New Restaurant. Its fusilli with octopus and bone marrow is now on MIKA’s dinner menu.

When did MIKA open and who owns it?

MIKA opened on December 12, 2024 at The Plaza Coral Gables. It is Chef Michael White’s first stand-alone restaurant in Florida, opened with partner Alex Pirez of Mocca Group and restaurant operator Matt Safchik.

What should I order at MIKA?

Start with the crudo, the lobster burrata or the grilled octopus. For pasta, the spaghetti with blue crab and Siberian sturgeon caviar and the fusilli with octopus and bone marrow are the signatures. For a main, the whole branzino, the lobster risotto and the bone-in veal parmigiana are the tableside favorites.

Does Michael White cook at MIKA?

Yes. White is based in South Florida and is often in the MIKA kitchen, cooking and teaching the team. He has said that working with young cooks and mentoring is the part of the job he values most.

What awards has Michael White won?

White’s restaurants have earned six Michelin stars in total, including two at Marea and one at Ai Fiori. Marea won the James Beard Award for Best New Restaurant in 2010, and White has been a James Beard nominee for Best Chef: New York. Marea is also a member of Relais & Châteaux.

Is MIKA Michael White’s first restaurant in Florida?

MIKA is his first stand-alone restaurant in Florida. He previously cooked at Lido at The Surf Club and opened Mirabella at the Fontainebleau in Miami Beach, and his mother is from Fort Pierce, Florida.

What does “truffles and blue jeans” mean?

It is White’s shorthand for MIKA’s approach: luxurious ingredients and precise cooking in a room where guests can relax. The restaurant is polished but not stiff, designed for regular visits rather than only special occasions.

Has Michael White written any cookbooks?

Yes. He published Fiamma: The Essence of Contemporary Italian Cooking in 2006 and Classico e Moderno: Essential Italian Cooking with Andrew Friedman in 2013.
