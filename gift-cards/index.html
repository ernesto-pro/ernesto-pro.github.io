Gift Cards

Some gifts are better eaten. Give a dinner at MIKA — crudo, caviar, handmade pasta and a table by the olive trees.

Evan Sung / Courtesy of MIKA

The gift

Give them a good evening.

A MIKA gift card is an invitation to sit down, order slowly and let the kitchen take care of the rest.

Cards are purchased online through Toast and delivered electronically. Redeem yours at the restaurant for food and beverage — whether that is a full dinner for two or aperitivo at the marble bar.

Buy a gift cardQuestions? Email us

Gift cards are issued and processed by Toast. For help with an existing card, contact the restaurant at +1 305 404 2200.

How it works

Three small steps.

01

Choose online

Select the amount and complete your purchase through Toast, MIKA’s secure gift card partner.

02

Send by email

The card arrives digitally, ready to forward to the recipient or print at home.

03

Redeem at MIKA

Use it toward dinner, lunch, brunch or aperitivo at 3007 Ponce de Leon Blvd.
